Kobiton: Product One-Pagers

A family of product one-pagers for Kobiton, a real-device mobile testing platform, built on one A4 template that explains any product on a single page.

Print & Sales Collateral
Editorial Layout
Template System
A4 & US Letter

The Challenge

Clear enough for a glance, deep enough for engineers

Sales needed leave-behinds for a growing list of products. Each had to be scannable in seconds, credible to technical buyers, and ready to print in A4 and US Letter.

The Kobiton + Applitools Eyes A4 page, the reference for the whole series.

My approach

One template, any product

I treated the one-pager as a product, not a poster: a fixed two-column grid, reusable components and strict section labels, with every page ending on the same purple call to action.

Nunito Sans Black for titles, Roboto Flex for copy, and a palette led by Kobiton purple.

One template, two audiences: setup steps for accessibility testing, a developer workflow for virtualUSB.

The densest topic: six deployment models and a simple guide to choosing one, explored in two versions.

Every page ships in both formats, with spacing adjusted rather than content cut.

The Result

A consistent family of one-pagers in A4 and US Letter, which the team can extend to any new product with the same template.
