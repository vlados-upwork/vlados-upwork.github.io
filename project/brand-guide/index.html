Scaled Cognition: Brand Guide

One visual system for a company building reliable AI. The same ink, blue and warm neutrals and the same DM Sans voice, carried from the website and the Agent Builder product to a whitepaper, a roll-up banner and a rubber stamp.

Brand Identity
Website & Product
Print & Merch

The Challenge

Scaled Cognition sells reliability. The brand had to feel precise and calm, closer to an engineering instrument than a hype-driven AI startup, while working across very different surfaces: a marketing site, a data-dense product, long-form research and event print.

Without shared foundations each surface drifted: different blues, different type, different tone. The goal was one guide a designer, an engineer or a print vendor could pick up and get right the first time.

A restrained palette: ink and navy for authority, one electric blue for action, sky and cyan for data and highlights, and warm off-whites instead of flat grey. DM Sans carries everything from 96px display to 12px legal; DM Mono is reserved for IDs, code and numbers.

Brand Guide

The homepage leads with one claim, super-reliable intelligence for CX, and proves it with product shots and customer outcomes. Large DM Sans display type, generous warm whitespace and a single blue call to action keep the message calm and confident.

Every page ships at 1440, 768 and 390. Sections stack instead of shrinking, calls to action stay thumb-reachable and display type steps down one size per breakpoint.

The Agent Builder runs on the same tokens as the site, extended with semantic surfaces, light and dark modes and a component library, the product feels like the website’s promise made real.

Long-form research gets an editorial treatment: a deep navy cover with the signal-wave motif, then clean white spreads with executive photography, pull figures and the blue accent bar for key findings. It uses the same blue, type and spacing as the website, so moving from the site to the PDF feels like one document. At events the roll-up banner asks one question, “What if models didn’t hallucinate?”, set large on ink and answered on electric blue, with a QR code inviting engineers to join the team.

“No Jell-O, firm foundations only.” A playful internal badge system for stickers and swag, shown here as a wooden rubber stamp. Nine variations reuse the brand blue, navy, cyan and violet.

The Result

One guide now covers every surface. Website, product, research and print share the same palette, type scale and tone of voice.

Designers ship faster because the decisions are already made, vendors get exact colour and type specs, and the brand reads as one confident company wherever someone meets it.
