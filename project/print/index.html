Print Collateral: Flyers, Guides and Forms

A selection of print work for clients in energy, healthcare, medical manufacturing, construction materials and business services: event flyers, a patient guide, a trade show handout, a product spec sheet and an order form.

Print Design
Marketing Collateral
Editorial Layout

The Challenge

Print gets one chance. A flyer at a trade show or a guide in a waiting room has a few seconds to be understood, has to work from an office printer as well as a press, and carries the brand without a screen to lean on.

Every piece here had a different reader: engineers deciding whether to attend a training, parents worried about their child, buyers walking a trade show floor, contractors comparing products, and clients filling in a long order form.

The Approach

I start every document from its reading order: the one message, the proof, then the details and the call to action. A clear grid, a strong headline, real photography and generous white space do most of the work, and every file is prepared for print.

The pieces below range from a single-page event flyer to a two-sided patient guide, a two-page product sheet and a dense order form, each laid out for the way it is read.

An event flyer for the 2016 Title 24 Energy Code Update training: the date and venue as prominent as the title.

A two-sided guide for parents from a psychology practice: a warm photo and question-led headings make a difficult topic approachable.

Both sides flat: the questions families ask, what an assessment covers and how to book one.

A trade show handout for a medical device manufacturer exhibiting at MTEC 2026: one message, the booth number and a QR code.

A two-page product sheet for a cold pour crack filler, and a long German order form kept readable at small sizes.

The Result

Each piece has a single job, a clear hierarchy and print-ready files, from office printers to offset presses.
