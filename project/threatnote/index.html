ThreatNote

A threat-intelligence workspace where security teams hunt threats, manage detection signatures and publish reports, designed in light and dark.

Product design
Design system
Light & dark mode
Website

The Challenge

Fast to scan, deep when it matters

Analysts jump between dashboards, hunts, detection rules and long-form reports all day. The interface had to stay calm and dense at the same time, and work in both light and dark rooms.

The previous ThreatNote interface the redesign started from: a crowded dashboard and pages that ran on far too long.

My approach

Three directions, one product

I started from a Shadcn baseline, then pushed it into a sharper “Techno” language with Geist type, purple accents and a dark mode built for long sessions.

The same dashboard in all three styles, from neutral baseline to the final light and dark themes.

A purple brand scale on cool neutrals, with Geist for interface type and Geist Mono for indicators and rules.

Built around an analyst’s day

From spotting a threat to hunting it, writing the rule that detects it and publishing what was learned.

A hunt keeps its scope, indicators and findings on one page, and a new hunt starts from a short guided form.

Each rule has three clear modes: read it, edit it in a code view, and share it with other teams or tools.

Reports, articles and stories share one editorial layout, with key takeaways and indicators surfaced above the full write-up.

One search reaches every hunt, rule and report, and grouped menus keep a growing product easy to move around.

Every screen ships in both themes, sharing the same layout, tokens and purple accent.

From a gap to a published story

A gap is only useful when someone acts on it. The story builder turns what Ask ThreatNote found into a brief for stakeholders, one block at a time: title and summary, a cover, the overview, indicators from the hunt, evidence images, a timeline and the links to actors and techniques. Then it goes out with a sharing level and a notification.

The prototype picks up where the coverage gap above leaves off, in Ask ThreatNote, and ends with the published story. It plays by itself, or click through it.

The Result

A consistent light and dark interface for hunting, detection and reporting, built on a shared token set so new screens stay on-brand.
