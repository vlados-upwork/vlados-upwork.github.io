Sophia AI: Clinical Knowledge Assistant

I designed Sophia, an AI assistant for a university hospital: staff ask questions about procedures, admins upload SOPs that the AI checks for conflicts, and every procedure becomes a training.

AI Product Design
Healthcare
Web, Mobile and Desktop

The Challenge

A hospital’s procedures live in hundreds of documents. They overlap, go out of date and sometimes contradict each other, and staff have no quick way to find the right answer at the bedside.

Training on a new procedure was separate work, tracked by hand, with certificates kept in folders.

The Approach

Sophia puts the knowledge base and the assistant side by side: the procedure on the left, answers and suggestions on the right. When an admin uploads an SOP, the AI suggests improvements, cross-checks it against every other procedure, and turns it into a training with questions.

The same system runs on desktop, on mobile and as a native desktop widget, and admins manage users, groups, certificates and statistics in one place. The hero follows one SOP from upload to a published training.

Ask Sophia next to the procedure: answers come from the hospital’s own documents and suggest the next question.

The training center: assigned courses, progress and what is left to complete.

Statistics for admins: completion by user, department and course, with AI insights.

Certificates and badges, built from the trainings they require.

On mobile: the assistant, open courses and AI suggestions on a new entry.

A native desktop widget, light and dark, to ask Sophia without opening the app.

The Result

One place for what the hospital knows, and proof that staff know it.

Conflicts are found when a procedure is uploaded, not on the ward, and every new SOP becomes a training without extra work.
