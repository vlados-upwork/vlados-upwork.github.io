Backed: Partnership Deck 2026

I redesigned the 2026 partnership deck for BACKED, a European venture capital firm: 25 slides that sell its year of events to brand partners, built on the BACKED brand guidelines.

Presentation Design
Editorial Layout
Brand System

The Challenge

The old deck had grown one slide at a time. Event pages were walls of uppercase text, layouts changed from slide to slide, and the same sponsorship list was repeated in full on every page.

Partners read it in a few minutes, often on a phone. They needed to find the event, the audience and the price at a glance.

The Approach

I rebuilt the deck on the brand system: Landour Display headlines, TT Tricks for reading, thin rules, the B mark and the flag, and the pink to yellow gradient kept for the one number or tier that matters on each slide.

Every event shares one template: title, a details line, one sentence, four benefits and the tiers as rows. A paragraph became a line, and light and dark slides alternate to give the deck a rhythm.

The cover: the BACKED wordmark, the year in the gradient and a photo from the events.

The mission in one line, three reasons to partner, and the numbers that prove the community shows up.

Who partners meet, as three short lists, and the brands that already partner with BACKED.

Photos from the rooms and the Founder Portal, where portfolio founders find trusted providers.

The whole year on one slide, seven series colour coded, then three steps to become a partner.

One template for every event: title, details, one sentence, benefits and the tiers as rows, with the top tier on the gradient.

The SuperReturn dinner and The Long Game retreat, one dark and one light slide.

Slush week in Helsinki: the LP reception and the BACKED Party.

The smaller series follow the same layout, from the Frontier Tech Dinner to The Culture Bet.

LONDON BIO and Capital Table: quarterly series with a single price or a full-series tier.

The new Fintech Forum and the Power Breakfast for female founders and investors.

The Founder Retreat: the goals and a partner quote, then three tiers as cards.

An annual portfolio package, and a partner quote to close the deck.

The Result

A deck that reads in minutes and looks like the firm behind it.

Thirteen events share one layout, so a new event is a copy and a few edits, and partners compare tiers at a glance.
