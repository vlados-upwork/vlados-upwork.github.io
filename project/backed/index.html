Backed: Brand Identity

Backed is a venture capital firm built to back bold founders. I created a living, moving identity for it: a flag-inspired brand mark, a chevron graphic language, a two-typeface system, a warm gradient palette and the guidelines that carry them across decks, social and the web.

The Challenge

Venture brands tend to look alike: cautious, corporate and interchangeable. Backed wanted an identity as bold and human as the founders it supports, and one that could grow with the firm as it became known globally.

It had to work everywhere at once: as a tiny favicon and a full-bleed event banner, in a formal investor deck and a casual social post, on owned channels and on platforms Backed does not control.

The Identity

Everything starts from one shape: a flag, standing for progress, unity and boldness. It forms the brand mark, sharpens the K in the wordmark and multiplies into the chevron pattern, so even a fragment of the system reads as Backed.

The brand mark joins the letter B with the flag shape. Built for constrained spaces, it works as an app icon, favicon, Slack avatar or business-card detail, and holds up on its own or inside a square or circle.

Four lockups cover every context. The full BACKED wordmark leads on non-owned channels such as events and press. The B-and-flag mark builds recognition on external platforms, and the condensed BKD lockup gives Backed’s own invitations, animations and decks a less formal, more approachable voice.

The chevron repeats the curved cut of the K into a pattern that suggests momentum. It scales from a single accent beside the mark to full-bleed textures, and takes the gradient palette on dark backgrounds for moments that need energy.

Two typefaces with clear roles. Landour, a wide geometric display face in four weights, carries headlines and the wordmark’s confidence. TT Tricks, a warm serif, is the workhorse for body copy, adding the human, editorial tone that sets Backed apart from typical tech brands.

Black and off-white form the foundation, with yellow as secondary support and pinks and oranges as focused accents. Each colour also extends into a vertical gradient, giving the chevron pattern, deck highlights and portrait backgrounds a warm, sunrise-like glow.

Portraits are where the brand meets people. Option A sets partners against the gradient chevron pattern, while Option D places them in front of an oversized B on a soft gradient, with name and title running vertically in Landour. Both turn a headshot into an unmistakably Backed asset.

The system scales to long-form documents. The investor deck index uses Landour at large sizes, a gradient pill to mark the current chapter and thin rules for structure, keeping a dense fund document clear and on-brand.

The website brings every element together under the line “Built to back the bold”. Full-bleed black sections, candid community photography, Landour headlines and rounded stat cards lead into portfolio companies, insights and news, closing with a giant BKD wordmark in the footer.

The Result

Backed now has a complete identity system: four logo lockups, a brand mark, a chevron pattern, two typefaces, a three-tier palette with gradients and portrait treatments, all documented in a 56-page brand guideline.

Because every element grows from the same flag shape, the brand stays recognisable whether it appears as a favicon, a partner portrait, an investor deck or a full website, and teams can create new assets without diluting it.
