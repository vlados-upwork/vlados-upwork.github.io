Lambdax: Contract Management

I designed Lambdax, a contract management platform for government contractors: agreements, line items, documents and approvals in one place, with filters and exports that keep up with thousands of records.

Product Design
B2B SaaS
Contract Management

The Challenge

Government contracting runs on long agreements with many line items, modifications and linked documents. Teams worked across spreadsheets and email, and finding one agreement, or the ten that answered a question, took too long.

Every customer also sets up agreements differently, so the product needed fields, sections and views that admins can shape without a developer.

The Approach

I started from the agreements list, the screen people live in. Quick filters, an advanced filter builder with rules and groups, saved table views, column resizing, favourites, bulk actions and export all work on the same table.

Each agreement opens on a dashboard with its status, overview, activity, linked documents and team, and the details hold line items section by section. The hero filters the list and exports it.

Each agreement opens on its own dashboard: status, overview, activity, linked documents and the team.

Line items section by section, edited in place with a clear saved state.

A home dashboard people make their own: a colour, and widgets dragged in from the side.

Admins configure agreement types: sections, fields and their technical names.

Settings on mobile: profile, preferences and search options.

The Result

Finding the right agreements went from a hunt to a filter.

Teams build the view they need, save it and export it in two clicks, while admins shape agreement types on their own.
