© 2012 - 2026 vlados

I design and build software that feels clear and simple to use.

I’m a freelance product designer and developer. Since 2012 I have helped B2B and SaaS teams turn complicated products into interfaces people understand at a glance.

My work covers research, information architecture, interaction design and polished UI, along with the design systems and brand identities that hold a product together. Recent projects include AI agent platforms, data security tools, a cloud phone system portal, event finance software and a smart thermostat with its companion app.

I also build what I design: websites in Webflow, WordPress or React, native Android and iOS apps in Kotlin and Swift, and hybrid apps in Flutter, React Native or Ionic. One person can take your idea from the first sketch to the release.

You work with me directly. There are no account managers and no handovers, just one person who learns your product in depth and either builds it or delivers organised files your developers can build from.

One person, from the first question to the launch

I work on my own on purpose. The person who asks the first questions about your product is the same person who designs the last screen, so nothing gets lost along the way.

I fit into the way your team already works: shared Figma files and repositories, regular check-ins and quick feedback, whether you are an early startup or an established company.

What I bring to every project

  • Clarity first

    Every screen should have one clear job. I remove the noise until the important information and the next action are obvious.

  • Craft in the details

    States, edge cases, spacing and copy get the same care as the main screens, because that is where a product starts to feel finished.

  • Honest collaboration

    I explain my decisions, listen to pushback and change course when the evidence says so. Good ideas can come from anyone on your team.

  • Built to last

    I design systems rather than isolated screens and write code that is easy to maintain, so your product stays consistent as it grows and new features are quicker to ship.

Good work doesn’t happen by chance. It comes from understanding the people who use a product and caring about every detail they touch.

My services

Clients

Clients I have worked with include Scaled Cognition, Dasera, PortaOne, EventWise, Ambiente, Kobiton, Backed, ThreatNote and Senna.

My approach

I start by understanding the product as it is today: the people who use it, the data behind it and the goals of the business. A short audit and a few conversations with your team save weeks of guessing later.

Copying competitors only makes a product look like theirs. I look for the decisions that make yours distinct and design around them, so it stands out for the right reasons.

Simple ideas work better than complicated ones. I reduce each flow to what people actually need, then make that part fast and obvious.

Your team knows your product and customers better than anyone. I work alongside your product managers and developers, share work early and often, and leave behind files and patterns they can keep using.

Good work deserves to be seen. I like to show the work I design for my clients, and I enjoy watching those products keep growing long after the project ends.

Have a product idea and nobody to design it? I’d love to help.
